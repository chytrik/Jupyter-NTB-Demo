Nexus Technologies — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)
Revision 2026-Q1

PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accruing at 2.083 days per month. PTO requests must be submitted via the NexusHR portal at least 5 business days in advance. Carry-over is limited to 5 unused days into Q1 of the following year. Employees in their first year receive a prorated allocation starting from their hire date.

PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken in up to two non-consecutive blocks within the first 12 months after birth or adoption. During parental leave, all benefits including stock vesting continue unchanged.

LEARNING BUDGET
Each employee receives an annual learning budget of EUR 3,000 for conferences, courses, certifications, and books. The budget resets on April 1st each year and does not carry over. Requests above EUR 500 require manager approval. Approved AI/ML certifications receive a 50% top-up (i.e., up to EUR 4,500 total for AI-related learning).

EXPENSE POLICY
Business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal (Nexus Travel). Economy class for flights under 6 hours, business class permitted for flights over 6 hours. Hotel per-diem: EUR 180 in Western Europe, EUR 120 in Eastern Europe, USD 250 in the United States. Meal allowance: EUR 50 per day. Receipts required for all expenses above EUR 25.

WORK FROM HOME
Nexus Technologies operates a "remote-first" policy. Employees may work from home up to 4 days per week. One mandatory in-office day per week is designated by each team lead (typically Tuesday or Wednesday). Employees living more than 100 km from the nearest office may apply for fully remote status. Home office equipment allowance: one-time EUR 1,500 upon joining.
