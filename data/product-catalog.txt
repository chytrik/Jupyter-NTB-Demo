Nexus Technologies — Product Catalog 2026

NEXUS SHIELD PRO
Enterprise endpoint protection platform. Supports Windows, macOS, and Linux. Uses behavioral AI engine "Sentinel v3" for zero-day threat detection. License: per-endpoint, minimum 500 seats. Annual subscription starts at $42 per endpoint. Includes 24/7 SOC monitoring and quarterly threat briefings. Deployment options: SaaS, on-premise, or hybrid. Current version: 8.4.1, released March 2026.

NEXUS VAULT
Hardware security module (HSM) appliance for key management. FIPS 140-3 Level 3 certified. Supports RSA-4096, ECDSA P-384, and post-quantum algorithms (ML-KEM-1024, ML-DSA-87). Maximum throughput: 15,000 signing operations per second. Form factor: 1U rackmount. Price: $28,500 per unit, volume discounts available for orders above 10 units. Requires NexusOS 3.x firmware.

NEXUS CLOUDWATCH
Cloud security posture management (CSPM) tool. Monitors AWS, Azure, GCP, and OpenShift environments. Checks against CIS Benchmarks, NIST 800-53, and PCI DSS v4.0. Generates compliance reports in PDF and machine-readable OSCAL format. Pricing: based on number of monitored cloud accounts, starting at $850/month for up to 5 accounts. API available for CI/CD integration.

NEXUS IDENTITY FABRIC
Unified identity governance platform. Integrates with Active Directory, Okta, Ping Identity, and LDAP directories. Features: automated access reviews, segregation of duties enforcement, privileged access management, and just-in-time provisioning. Supports SCIM 2.0 and SAML 2.0. Enterprise license: $15 per managed identity per month.
