Nexus Technologies — Information Security Policy
Version 4.2 | Effective: January 2026

1. Password Requirements
All employees must use passwords with a minimum length of 16 characters, including at least two uppercase letters, two digits, and two special characters. Passwords expire every 90 days. The last 12 passwords cannot be reused. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems classified as Tier 1 or Tier 2.

2. Data Classification
Nexus Technologies uses four data classification levels:
- PUBLIC: Marketing materials, published research, job postings.
- INTERNAL: Internal memos, meeting notes, org charts.
- CONFIDENTIAL: Customer data, financial reports, source code, API keys.
- RESTRICTED: Cryptographic keys, penetration test results, board minutes, M&A documents.

3. Incident Response
Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 30 minutes of detection. The SOC hotline is ext. 4477 or soc@nexus-tech.internal. All Tier 1 incidents trigger automatic notification to the CISO and Legal department. Post-incident reviews must be completed within 5 business days.

4. Remote Access
VPN access requires a hardware token (YubiKey 5 series) plus biometric verification. Split tunneling is prohibited. All remote sessions are recorded and retained for 180 days. Contractors are limited to the Contractor VPN profile which restricts access to project-specific subnets only.

5. Acceptable Use
Company devices must not be used for cryptocurrency mining, personal cloud storage synchronization, or running unauthorized virtual machines. The IT department conducts quarterly audits of installed software. Violations result in a formal warning on first offense and device confiscation on second offense.
